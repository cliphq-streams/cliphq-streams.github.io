Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

ClipHQ ("we") runs clipforge, a private publishing tool we use to post short videos to ClipHQ's own social media accounts on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and X. This policy explains what clipforge accesses when an account is connected to it, and what we do with that information.

Who uses clipforge

clipforge is not a public product. Only accounts owned or managed by ClipHQ are connected to it. We don't offer sign-up to the general public.

Information we access

When a ClipHQ account is connected, the platform gives clipforge an access token and the permissions we request. Depending on the platform, that can include:

  • Basic account information, such as the account ID, username and profile picture.
  • Permission to upload and publish videos to that account.
  • Information about videos we've published, such as status, view counts and other public engagement numbers.

We do not access private messages, contacts, payment information or other people's accounts.

How we use it

We use this information only to publish ClipHQ's videos to the connected account and to see how those videos perform. We do not sell, rent or share it with anyone, and we don't use it for advertising.

Google user data

clipforge's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

Access tokens and video data are stored on ClipHQ's own computer, not on a shared server, and are only used by clipforge. We keep them only as long as the account stays connected.

Removing access and deleting data

You can disconnect clipforge at any time from the platform's own settings: TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions), Google (myaccount.google.com/permissions), or Facebook and Instagram (Settings → Business integrations). Once access is removed, we delete the stored tokens. To ask us to delete anything else, email yuripat999@gmail.com.

Children

clipforge is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
